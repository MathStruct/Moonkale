The graph view resets when the window gets resized or another window gets drawn over it.

**Done 2026-09-20.** The panel reloads its graph whenever an input changes — the active document, the index epoch, the filters — and on desktop a focus change or a relayout of the window can trigger such a reload; until now every `set_graph` discarded the positions, restarted the force layout from the spiral and refitted the camera, which looked like a reset. Now `GraphView::set_graph` is **incremental**: when the new graph shares at least half its nodes with the current one, known nodes keep their positions and pins, only new nodes are placed (at the mean of their known neighbours, nudged apart), the layout is *warmed* (temperature 3) instead of restarted — and not even that when nothing changed — and the camera is left exactly where the user put it. A graph with nothing in common (another folder, a database schema) still starts fresh with a fit. The earlier resize rule stays: a view the user never panned or zoomed re-fits when its canvas is resized (P-091); a touched view does not. Verified in `graph3d.mjs`: zoom, change the active document (a reload), camera identical afterwards.
