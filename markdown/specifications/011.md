title: "Spec 011 — Every panel closeable"
tags: [spec, shell]
status: open
---
Graph, Agent, Terminal, Links, Git, History, Search, Explorer and Settings are `closable: false` today (`PanelContribution.closable` in each extension), so their tabs have no ×. Wanted: an × on all of them — closing a panel just removes it from its tile; nothing is lost because none of them holds unsaved state (the terminal asks first if a process is running).

**Behaviour**: every panel is closeable; reopening is via the activity bar / *View → Show ▸* / the palette (`view.panel.<id>`) — [[009]]. When a tile becomes empty it collapses (the side bar folds, the bottom panel hides) and the neighbouring split takes the space; *Reset Layout* restores the defaults. The last main-tile panel closing leaves the empty "open something" tile as now. The phone shell keeps its bottom bar regardless.

**Change**: `closable: true` in all contributions (the `on_panel_closed` hook already exists in the shell), the empty-tile collapse in `shell.rs`, and `view.panel.*` commands for every panel (not just five). E2E: close Graph, reopen from the View menu; close Terminal with a running process → prompt.
