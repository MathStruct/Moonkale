- Inserting an element into the flow editor currently is somewhat problematic for editing the vaulues. If I click on the filed and press <-- or entf the element gets deleted.

**Done 2026-09-19.** The parameter fields inside a block now keep their keys and pointer events to themselves: Backspace/Delete edit the text instead of deleting the block, arrows move the caret instead of nudging the block, Enter/Space no longer toggle the selection, and clicking into a field no longer starts a drag. Ctrl/Meta combos (Ctrl+S) still reach the editor. Change: `packages/editors/flow/src/panel.rs` (`mk-flow-params` stops `keydown` unless Ctrl/Meta, and `pointerdown`). Covered by a step in `packages/web/tests/e2e/flow.mjs`.
