---
title: "Spec 006 — Android graph: pinch to zoom, two fingers to rotate"
tags: [spec, android, graph]
status: done
---
The Graph window in Android does currently not allow for zoom in/out or rotation with two finger gesture.

**Done 2026-09-20.** The renderer's pointer handlers (`graph-render/src/web.rs`) now track touch pointers (with pointer capture, `pointercancel` handled): a second finger starts `Drag::Pinch` — the distance between the fingers zooms at their midpoint (`Camera::zoom_at`, so in 3D it dollies), the midpoint's motion pans, and in 3D the fingers' rotation turns the camera's yaw. Lifting one finger continues as a one-finger pan without a click; a node picked up by the first finger stays pinned. `GraphView::camera_state()` exports `[scale, cx, cy, yaw, pitch, dist]` for tests. Verified in Chromium with CDP touch events (`graph3d.mjs`: 2D pinch ×4, 3D rotation 1.2 rad with the distance stable) and on the Galaxy S10e through the WebView's DevTools socket (`packages/web/tests/e2e/android-pinch.mjs`: scale 1.6 → 8.0, yaw 0.6 → −0.4). Note: 2D has no rotation — the canvas has no orientation to rotate; two fingers pan and zoom there.
